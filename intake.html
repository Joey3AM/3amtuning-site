Vehicle Readiness Checklist

Please verify before your appointment — unready vehicles may incur a reschedule fee.

  • No active check engine codes (or you've told me what they are)
  • Battery healthy and charging system good (13.8V+ running)
  • Fuel system functional (no fuel pressure issues, working injectors)
  • No major vacuum or exhaust leaks
  • Cooling system working (no overheating issues)
  • All modifications fully installed and wired (no half-finished installs)
  • If remote: you have an HP Tuners MPVI interface (MPVI2/3/4) and a Windows laptop
