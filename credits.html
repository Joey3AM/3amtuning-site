What this is: HP Tuners charges credits and sometimes an unlock service to license your vehicle's computer for custom tuning. The cost depends on your platform and year — it ranges from $100 to over $2,900. This tool shows you exactly what your vehicle needs, at HP Tuners' actual prices. How to read this

ECM credits license the engine computer. Most GM vehicles need 2 or 4 credits; newer Global B platforms need 6-10.

TCM credits license the transmission computer (when it's separate). 8/10-speed transmissions with a separate TCM typically need 4 credits. 6-speed (6L80/6L90) trans tuning is included in your calibration at no extra charge — but the TCM may still need its own credits to license.

Unlock service is a one-time HP Tuners bench fee for newer platforms (2019+). Classic GM (pre-2019) has no unlock — you just license and tune. Global B (2022.5+) requires sending the module to HP Tuners, which means vehicle downtime.

Important notes

  • "Has it ever been tuned?" matters. A vehicle previously tuned with HP Tuners custom software is already unlocked — the unlock service fee disappears. A handheld programmer (Range, Hypertech, Pulsar) does NOT unlock the module.
  • 2015-16 T87 (first-year 8-speed): TCM rides on the ECM's 2 credits — $0 TCM credits on these specific years.
  • 2017+ T87A and 2019+ T93: TCM upgrade is now FREE via OBDII (no send-in). Just 4 credits to license.
  • Global B (2022.5+): TCM still requires $399.99 send-in service + 4 credits.
  • Remote tuning: you purchase credits directly from HP Tuners on your own VCM account. The numbers here are what you'll pay — we don't mark them up.
  • Drop-off tuning: we purchase the credits on our interface and pass them through at cost on your invoice.
