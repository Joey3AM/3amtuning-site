PRE-TUNE CHECKLIST

Run through this before tune day — mobile, drop-off, or remote. Printable.
Why this matters: calibration is not a repair. A tune can't fix a mechanical problem — it can only hide it or make it worse. Every box you check here is time on tune day spent tuning instead of diagnosing.

Ignition

  • Spark plugs are the right heat range and gap for the combination (boosted and nitrous combos usually need a colder plug, gapped down).
  • Plugs, coils, and wires are in known-good condition — no random misfires.

Fuel system

  • Fuel filter is fresh (if the vehicle has a serviceable one).
  • Fuel pump can support the power goal — a stock pump doesn't feed a big cam and boost.
  • Injector part number / flow data is known and disclosed if not stock.
  • E85 / flex-fuel: working ethanol content sensor installed and lines are ethanol-rated.
  • The tank has the fuel the tune is being built for — fresh, from a good station.

Air path

  • Air filter is clean; MAF sensor is clean and reading.
  • No vacuum leaks — check hoses, fittings, intake gaskets.
  • Boosted: clamps tight, couplers healthy, no boost leaks (pressure-test if you can).

Engine health

  • No active misfires and no unresolved trouble codes — if a light is on, tell us what and why before tune day.
  • Oil level correct, oil in good condition, no significant leaks.
  • If there's any doubt about a cylinder, do the compression / leak-down test before scheduling.

Cooling & charging

  • Coolant level correct, fans working — tuning involves repeated load pulls at operating temperature.
  • Battery healthy and charging system working. Flashing a module with weak voltage can brick it; remote customers should keep a maintainer on the battery during writes.

Drivetrain

  • Transmission fluid level and condition good — required if transmission calibration is part of the job.
  • Tires in safe condition and properly inflated (they carry the load testing).
  • No driveline vibrations or slipping that "only happens sometimes." Tell us about it.

Tune day (mobile / drop-off)

  • At least half a tank of the agreed fuel.
  • Any parts going on the vehicle are ON the vehicle — tune day is not install day.
  • Plan for the session: drop-off runs are typically 4-5 hours.

Found a problem going through this list? That's the checklist working — tell us and we'll figure out the order of operations together. Diagnostics is a service we offer too.
